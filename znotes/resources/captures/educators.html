WHAT TEACHERS SAY

"76% of teachers actively use ZNotes to prepare and deliver classroom content."

Our content is always accurate and up to date.

Here's how we make sure of it.

01

Application

High-achieving students including World Toppers and Olympiad winners submit applications from all over the world.

02

Interviews

We review applications and verify academic qualifications. Selected students are interviewed by our Content team.

03

Training

Contributors complete an onboarding process and a self-paced training program on how to write the best notes.

04

Co-creation

Each contributor is assigned to a member of the ZNotes team who supports the content development process over a 4–6 week period.

05

Moderation

Our content moderators review the final draft of the notes in order to ensure accuracy and alignment with the latest exam syllabus.

06

Publication

After final approval, the notes are made publicly accessible. We continuously review the notes and allow users to suggest improvements.

07

Expert Review

In some cases, educators with years of teaching experience will review the content and share feedback for improvements. Learn more and apply to be an expert reviewer.
